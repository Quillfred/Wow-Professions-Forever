## cMaNGOS Classic database (tools/vendor_factions.json, tools/npcs.json)

Derived from https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db (GPL v3): vendor lists, trainer
lists, NPC spawns and transports of Classic WoW, used as a fallback where
AllTheThings has no Forever data yet.
