- Learn level = the "Requires <Profession> (N)" line on the spell page.
- Source = the spell page's "Taught by" lists (NPC / item / quest) and the source data for the pattern item.
- Comments: Wowhead shows comments from every game version on these pages and doesn't mark which version a comment is about. Only comments dated 2026 (after Forever launched) were treated as Forever comments.
- "Agrees with" options: ATT, Wowhead export, neither, can't tell.

## Summary

- **Counts:** Wowhead export 68 of 68 (Part 1: 25 of 25; Part 2: 43 of 43). ATT 0, neither 0, can't tell 0.
- **ATT partly right:** 4508 and 19068, where Wowhead lists ATT's pattern item alongside the other source.
- **Learn levels:** ATT is higher by an amount that depends on the profession, but it is not one fixed number.
  - Blacksmithing: +25 on most rows, +20 on Truesilver Breastplate and Truesilver Gauntlets.
  - Leatherworking: +25, except Tanning Rack at +15.
  - Tailoring: +15, except Bolt of Runecloth and Simple Black Dress at +5.
  - No Forever comment says what level the in-game trainer actually asks for.
- **"Not released yet" in ATT:** every one has a real vendor or drop on Wowhead's Forever spell page (Blackrock Depths, Dire Maul, Zul'Gurub's Rin'wosho, Cenarion Circle's Mishta, Timbermaw's Meilosh). None of the spell pages marks one as unavailable. The pattern item pages were not opened, so an availability note there would have been missed.
- **Specialization:** Wowhead shows no specialization requirement on any of the 5 rows ATT flags (20874, 20872, 19085, 20853, 19068).
- **Chain set plans (1252239–1252251):** ATT says trainer, and Wowhead says Drop with no named mob. Forever players in the comments can't find them at trainers or vendors, and one got Protector's Chain Shirt from a Ragefire Chasm boss.

## Gaps / caveats

- Part 1 pages: the 2026 (Forever) comments were not read.
- Pattern item pages were not opened. Sources come from the spell page's embedded data, so a few vendor NPCs are unnamed.
- Zone names were inferred from Wowhead zone IDs (33 = Stranglethorn Vale, 1584 = Blackrock Depths, 2557 = Dire Maul, 1377 = Silithus, 361 = Felwood).
