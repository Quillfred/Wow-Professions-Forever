# SkillRoute: Classic data checked against WoW Forever (Wowhead Forever, 2026-11-23)

**Method.** I read 445 Wowhead Forever pages (`https://www.wowhead.com/forever/<type>=<id>`) one at a time, 10-16 seconds apart. Every page title ended in "- WoW Forever", and there were no errors or rate limits. The data comes from the Listview tables embedded in each page (Sold by, Teaches, Dropped by, Fished in, Disenchanted from, NPCs, Fishing), plus each page's map pins and its comments dated 2026. I didn't use AllTheThings.

**Is it Forever data or imported Classic?** Forever data. Pages include Forever-only NPCs (e.g. 257004), Forever-only recipe spells (e.g. 1230117), Forever-only zones (Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Mount Hyjal) and disenchant counts in the tens of thousands. Comments dated 2026 are sparse, and I've quoted them in the notes where they matter.

**Faction letters.** A = friendly to Alliance, H = friendly to Horde, as Wowhead marks them. Neutral counts as usable.
