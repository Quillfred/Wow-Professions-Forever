## 1. Enchanting trainers

**Rule used** (from the Tailoring check): a trainer teaches only the rank below its title, and offers nothing until your skill reaches that rank's threshold.
- Wowhead's [Enchanting guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/enchanting/overview) lists Expert-titled trainers as teaching "Apprentice, Journeyman", Artisan-titled as teaching "Expert", and Annora (Master) as teaching Artisan. That fits the rule.
- It also lists Journeyman-titled trainers as teaching "Apprentice, Journeyman", but their Wowhead Teaches tabs show Apprentice only, so I went with Apprentice.

Rank thresholds, from Wowhead's [Professions Overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/overview) and the Enchanting guide: Journeyman 50 skill / level 10; Expert 125 / level 20; Artisan 225 / level 35 (see Q5 for the 200 vs 225 question).

**Answers:**
- **Expert Enchanting for Horde: Hgarth (Stonetalon) is the only Horde trainer.** Kitta Firewind is Alliance-only. Wowhead lists no other Artisan-titled Enchanter, and none in a capital or on Zephras Isle. Your player (skill 130, level 29) meets the Expert requirements, so Hgarth should train them now (Inferred).
- **Artisan Enchanting: only Annora** (Wowhead guide: she "teaches Artisan Enchanting to both factions"). No other Artisan trainer is listed.
  - **Spawn condition:** she "only spawns after you clear out the surrounding scorpion trash (about 10 level 35-36 non-elite mobs)" (Wowhead Enchanting guide).
  - **Quest or key:** none mentioned in any source (not found).
  - **Getting there:** a 2026-11-20 comment says you clear Revelosh (main entrance) or the Obsidian Sentinel (rear entrance) plus trash. Another 2026-11-20 comment says Uldaman is "supposed to be 41" and calls her "difficult or impossible to reach without help".
- **Mob levels near Annora** (Wowhead's [Uldaman](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=1337) NPC list; the dungeon is now level 42-52):
  - Cleft Scorpid 35-36 (normal).
  - Deadly Cleft Scorpid 42-43 (normal).
  - Venomlash Scorpid 39-40 (elite).
  - Revelosh 40 (elite) and Obsidian Sentinel 42 (elite) at the two entrances.
  - Stonevault trolls 35-45 (mostly elite).
  - Annora herself: level 42 elite.
- **Trainer quirks:** none like Blacksmithing's for Enchanting. The only reports are that Hgarth is hard to find and Annora is hard to reach.
- **Recipes:** Hgarth, Kitta and Annora list Enchanting recipes up to skill 225 (Annora up to 250). The capital trainers stop at 150.

## 2. Faction-only patterns and the auction house

- **Is the auction house shared? No, except at the goblin towns.** In [ConquestCapped's AH guide](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-auction-house/) (updated 2026-11-16, in-game testing):
  - The capital auction houses are per faction, and a Horde character sees only Horde listings.
  - The neutral auction houses in Gadgetzan, Booty Bay and Everlook are shared by both factions. They take a 15% cut, against 5% at faction houses, and charge five times the deposit.
  - Its 15 Nov entry briefly said the capitals were cross-faction; the 16 Nov update reversed that.
- **Can faction-only vendor patterns be listed there? Unclear.**
  - ConquestCapped doesn't say.
  - A 2026 comment on Wowhead's Tailoring guide (liraelskye, about 13 days ago) says faction-specific patterns "CANNOT be sold on the combined auction house like they could in classic". Unconfirmed.
  - Wowhead marks [Recipe: Cooked Crab Claw (2698)](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2698) "Side: None" (not faction-locked), sold only by Kendor Kabonka (Alliance, unlimited), and it isn't bind-on-pickup.
  - Wowhead also shows it as a rare world drop in Westfall: Harvest Watcher 85 of 99,122 kills (~0.09%), Defias Smuggler 52 of 59,622, Harvest Golem 20 of 36,543.
- **Classic (not confirmed for Forever):** Classic players moved Alliance-only cooking recipes to Horde through the neutral auction house.
- **Which items are affected:** not found.
- **Suggestion for the addon:** don't tell Horde players to buy Alliance-only patterns "on the AH". At most say "neutral auction house (Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Everlook), if listed". Mention the Westfall drop for Cooked Crab Claw.

## 3. Zul'Gurub, Zandalar Tribe and the True Believer turn-ins

- **Zul'Gurub: no announcement it's open.**
  - Blizzard's [Forever raid overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24303009) (Oct 20) names only Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair, unlocking December 9, and doesn't mention Zul'Gurub.
  - The [icy-veins roadmap](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/roadmap) (Sep 13) lists Spring and Summer 2027 updates without naming raids, apart from a "revamped iconic raid" in Summer.
  - Wowhead Forever has a [Zul'Gurub zone page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=1977) (raid, level 60, 10/20 players, 82 NPCs) but no 2026 comments.
  - [Rin'wosho the Trader](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14921) is in Wowhead's Forever data, selling the Zandalar recipes, but no 2026 comments.
  - **Verdict:** Zul'Gurub exists in the game data but isn't announced as open. Don't route players to Zandalar recipes yet. When it opens: not found.
- **True Believer turn-ins: live, but the texts barely drop.** Encrypted Twilight Text (item 20404) is an objective of Secret Communication, Encrypted Twilight Texts, True Believers and Still Believing ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=20404)). 2026 comments:
  - 2026-11-16/17: "killed over 50 various Twilight dudes … and got zero".
  - 2026-11-17: "can confirm no text drop".
  - 2026-11-18 and 11-21: still no drop after the reset and update.
  - 2026-11-17 and 11-18: they reportedly drop only when you're in a party.

  Wowhead has some recorded drops: Twilight Avenger 12 of 280 kills, Twilight Geolord 10 of 301.

  **Verdict:** the quests exist. The texts may only drop in a group, which may be a bug. Warn players.
- **Pattern: Girdle of Insight (18514):** Wowhead Forever lists no source and it has no 2026 comments. Not found.

## 5. Artisan rank: skill 200 or 225?
Not settled by any Blizzard source.
- **225:** Wowhead's Professions Overview and Enchanting guide tables say "Min. Skill to Train" 225 for Artisan. RankedBoost's Forever guides say "train Artisan at 225".
- **200:** Method, icy-veins (2026-09-29) and wow-professions say 200. A 2026-11-20 comment on Annora says Artisan Enchanting needs "enchanting 200, level 35, and 5 gold". Players report Timothy Worthington offering training at 200 (2026-11-14 to 11-20).
- **Caveats:** the reply on Annora's page says that information "hasn't changed since the 2005 Thottbot comment", so it may be Classic knowledge, not tested in Forever. The Timothy reports cover his recipe list opening at 200, not the Artisan rank-up itself.
- **Confidence:** player reports lean towards 200, but nobody says they learned the rank at 200.
- **Suggestion for the addon:** treat 200 as "trainer opens"; tell the player to try at 200 and fall back to 225.
