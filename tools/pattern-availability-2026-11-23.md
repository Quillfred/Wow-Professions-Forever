# Are these 26 recipe patterns obtainable in WoW Forever today? (results)

- Checked: 2026-11-23, on Wowhead's WoW Forever item pages (`https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=<ID>`). All 26 pages loaded with titles ending in "- Item - WoW Forever".
- Comments: only those dated 2026 count. Wowhead mixes in older comments without labels.
- Prices are shown as Wowhead lists them on the "Sold by" tab.
- Drop/contained-in figures are Wowhead's count/out-of numbers. Wowhead doesn't say whether these come from Forever looting data or were imported from Classic.

## Headline

**No item page has a Forever availability marker.** There is no "not available to players", "Not yet available" or "Unavailable"/"Removed" tag. Every page checked says "Added in patch 1.60.1" (build 1.60.1.69876), which is the Forever launch build. The same line also appears on the Dire Maul, Blackrock Depths, Zandalar Tribe and Cenarion Circle pages, so it doesn't separate live content from unreleased content.

Two pages contained warning words, but both came from old imported Classic-era comments, not Forever markers:
- **22308:** "not available in Classic phase 1", from a 2019 comment.
- **20547:** "Removed", from an old comment subject about patch 1.10.

**No Forever page or 2026 comment confirms anyone getting any of these patterns.** The only 2026 comment on any of the 26 pages is a question: whether 22309 drops in Forever.

So the verdicts below rest on whether the source content is open. Wowhead's Forever pages don't say which old content is live, so for that I also used non-Wowhead sources, which are named.

## Summary

- **Available now: 18.**
  - Dire Maul: 10 (18655, 18515, 18516, 18414, 22309, 18418, 18415, 18416, 18417, 18487).
  - Blackrock Depths vendors: 5 (13483, 19444, 20754, 20755, 22308).
  - Argent Dawn: 1 (19446).
  - Cenarion Circle / Mishta: 2 (22310, 22312).
- **Not yet in Forever: 0.** Nothing on Wowhead's Forever pages says any of these is unavailable.
- **Can't tell: 8.**
  - Zandalar Tribe / Rin'wosho: 4 (20012, 20013, 20011, 20014).
  - True Believer Clippings / Silithus: 3 (20548, 20547, 20546).
  - Girdle of Insight, no source listed: 1 (18514).
- **What decides most of them:**
  - Dire Maul and Blackrock Depths cover 15 of the 18 "available now". Both have Forever zone pages, and a WoW Forever wiki listing names them among Forever's existing dungeons.
  - Zandalar reputation decides the 4 Rin'wosho recipes. If the Zul'Gurub raid isn't live, they can't be bought. The published roadmap has raids unlocking Dec 9 and doesn't name ZG.
- **ATT vs Wowhead:** ATT's "unreleased" tag looks wrong for the Dire Maul and Blackrock Depths patterns. It may be right, for now, for the 4 Zandalar recipes.
- **Vendors edited for Forever:** several vendors have Forever-specific stock or reputation changes on Wowhead (Rin'wosho, Lokhtos, Kania, Mishta, the Argent Dawn quartermasters). That suggests Blizzard set them up deliberately for Forever, but it isn't proof they're reachable today.

## Gaps / caveats

- **No direct confirmation:** no 2026 comment on any of the 26 pages confirms a purchase or drop. The "available now" verdicts depend on the content being open, not on a player saying they got the item.
- **Non-Wowhead sources:** Wowhead's Forever overview guide and its zone and faction pages don't say which classic content is live. For that I used warcraft.wiki.gg's WoW Forever page (dungeon list) and the published roadmap (insider-gaming.com, skycoach.gg: raids unlock Dec 9).
- **Pages not checked:** Silithus, the Plaguelands and Argent Dawn weren't checked on a Forever page.
- **Patch line not captured:** for 20013, 20011, 20014, 13483, 19446, 19444 and 20754, because of a parser bug on the first pass. Every page where it was captured says 1.60.1.
- **Drop counts:** these may be imported Classic data, not Forever loot data.
- **Possible rep discounts:** some prices look like they already include one (e.g. 5g 29s 41c, 2g 85s 88c). They're listed exactly as Wowhead shows them.
